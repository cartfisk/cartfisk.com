Carter's Egg Bake - Recipe

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup Top the Tater®
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup all-purpose baking mix (Bisquick™)
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon dijon mustard
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 lb. Jimmy Dean pork sausage
  • 4 green onions
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, diced

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350ºF. Spray a 9x13" pan with cooking spray or thinly coat with oil.

  2. Combine the eggs, Top the Tater®, milk, baking mix, cheese, mustard, Worcestershire, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Whisk or use hand mixer to mix on low speed, just until combined.

  3. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add sausage and cook until browned, breaking it into small pieces with a wooden spoon as it cooks. Drain grease and add sausage to bowl with the egg mixture.

  4. Add bell peppers and onion to the same skillet and sauté for 2-3 minutes. Add to the bowl with the eggs and gently stir everything to combine.

  5. Pour mixture into greased 9x13" pan and bake at 350ºF for 35-50 minutes or until the edges are set and the center is just barely jiggly.

  6. Serve/eat with hot sauce. Leftover egg casserole can be stored in the fridge and enjoyed within 3-4 days. Leftovers can be reheated in the microwave.
